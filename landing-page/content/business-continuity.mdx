## Built for the Long Term

ProfitPilot is an open-source project focused on providing reliable AI-powered business automation and analytics capabilities. The project is developed with an emphasis on maintainability, transparency, and long-term usability.

The source code is publicly available, allowing users and contributors to review implementation details, report issues, and participate in ongoing improvements.

## Open Source and Transparency

ProfitPilot is developed in the open, with its codebase, issue tracking, and development discussions available through the project's repository.

This approach promotes:

* Transparency in development
* Community participation and feedback
* Long-term maintainability
* Flexibility for customization and self-hosting where supported

## Business Continuity

The project is designed with continuity and sustainability in mind. Development focuses on maintaining a stable and reliable platform while continuously improving functionality and addressing reported issues.

As an open-source project, the availability of the source code helps reduce dependency on any single individual or organization and enables the community to continue supporting and extending the software over time.

## Project Commitment

ProfitPilot aims to provide a dependable platform for businesses, developers, and contributors. Ongoing maintenance efforts focus on software quality, usability, security improvements, and long-term project health.

Community contributions, issue reports, documentation improvements, and feature enhancements all play an important role in the continued development of the project.
